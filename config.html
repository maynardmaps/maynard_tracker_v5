Position share

Configure how website visitors can share their GPS location with others viewing the map. Users can enter a 4-letter display name, and their position will update at the configured interval and persist for the specified duration.

How long shared positions remain on the map after the last update. Range: 10–120 minutes. Default: 20.

How often a sharing user's GPS location is polled and sent. Range: 1–60 minutes. Default: 5.

Automatic shutdown time limit for position sharing sessions. Range: 1–24 hours. Default: 8. Prevents accidental long-running background sharing.
